How to play

  • 👆 Drag back from the ball like a slingshot, then release — the driver launches it high.
  • 🏌️ Pick your club — Driver (~300 yd), 5i (~200), 7i (~160), 9i (~135), Wedge (~100), Putter. The game suggests one each shot.
  • 🎯 On the green the putter takes over, Coffee Golf style — soft touch wins.
  • 🎯 The dotted line shows your aim — longer drag = more power.
  • 💨 Watch the wind — the dotted line flies straight, but the ball drifts in the air.
  • 💧 Water costs +1 stroke and drops you near where it splashed.
  • 🏖️ Sand grabs the ball — plan to carry it or play short.
  • 🕳️ Get it in the cup in as few strokes as you can. Beat par for 3 stars!
  • 🗓️ The daily challenge is 9 holes, same for everyone each day. Keep your streak alive.
  • 🗺️ The minimap (top right) shows the whole hole — follow the red arrow when the pin is off-screen.
  • 🖐️ Drag anywhere else to move the camera · twist two fingers to rotate · slide two fingers up/down to tilt the view · pinch or +/− to zoom · 🏠 opens the menu — pause, restart the hole, reset the camera, or quit.
